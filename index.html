Actions

Superpower only.

Your desktop, mouse and keyboard keep working as they do now. SUPERHERO adds five more ways in.

  • Look

    Working · improving

    A red laser dot follows your gaze, drawn above every window. Clicks pass straight through it.

  • Wink

    Working

    Winks, blinks, a nod or a head tilt become clicks, drags and scrolls. You choose which gesture does what.

  • Speak

    In testing

    Hold your mouth open for a second, then talk. Dictate text, or say “copy”, “scroll down”, “switch to Firefox”.

  • Hand

    In testing

    Pinch to click, two fingers to right click. An open palm or a fist can trigger anything else.

  • Wand

    Experiment

    Hold an ordinary pen like a magic wand and point with its tip. An early prototype for finer aim.

Working: verified on real hardware. In testing: built and covered by tests, not yet confirmed live. Experiment: a prototype. Roadmap →

Run it

Reveal the hero inside you!

Needs Python 3.12 and uv. The face and speech models download on first launch; after that it works offline.

$ git clone https://github.com/ninja7v/superhero
$ cd superhero
$ uv sync
$ uv run superhero
  1. Camera. Check that you can see yourself.
  2. Calibrate. Follow the dot with your eyes and keep your head still.
  3. Turn control on. Allow the system’s permission prompt.
  4. Go. Look at something and wink. To speak, hold your mouth open for a second.

Best results. Even light on your face and a camera at the top centre of the screen help most. Recalibrate after you move.

On Wayland, run uv run superhero install-desktop once so the emergency shortcut works. uv run superhero doctor shows what your machine supports.
